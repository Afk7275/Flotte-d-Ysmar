# ⚓ Flotte d'Ysmar

**A naval simulation and tactical strategy game set in the Mediterranean Sea.**

Flotte d'Ysmar puts you in command of a naval force operating in a dynamic Mediterranean environment. Navigate your ships, manage missions, communicate with other vessels, and make tactical decisions as you lead your fleet through its campaign.

## 🎮 Features

- **Story mode, season 1 "Sillage"** — A full narrative campaign: a prologue, ten chapters and an epilogue. Your grandfather's name weighs heavy in Toulon, a rival officer wants the frigate you are after, a journalist wants your quotes, and a secret from 1987 resurfaces. Dialogue scenes let you answer in a calm, frank or fiery tone; your choices shape your temperament, the admiral's trust, your rivalry, your family and your follower count, and they change later scenes, missions, press front pages and the ending. Your progress is in the "★ Story" panel.
- **Start in the headquarters** — The game now opens inside the staff headquarters, and the story prologue plays in the admiral's office.
- **Advanced training** — A full day aboard a borrowed frigate (from the harbour office, and chapter 1 of the story): game speed, chart and autopilot, fast transit, operations room, flying the helicopter to a buoy and landing it, launching the RHIB, walking to the bridge, reporting by message and taking a photo.
- **Better visuals** — Rounded characters with faces and hands, drifting volumetric clouds, sun glare and lens flare that the terrain and weather can hide, richer colour grading and a new loading screen.
- **Naval operations** — Control your ships and navigate through the Mediterranean.
- **Campaign system** — Complete missions and progress through a broader operational storyline, or follow the story mode.
- **Multiple vessel types** — Command a fleet featuring different surface ships and a submarine.
- **Tactical combat** — Track targets, manage weapons, and make decisions during engagements.
- **Navigation systems** — Use the radar, tactical map, compass, and helm controls.
- **Fleet communications** — Exchange messages with other ships and characters through an in-game communication system.
- **Port management** — Access port facilities and manage aspects of your naval operations.
- **Ship systems** — Monitor vessel status and respond to operational incidents.
- **Immersive interface** — A naval command interface designed to make you feel like you're operating a real fleet.
- **Five home ports** — Toulon, Ajaccio, Cagliari, Bizerte and Algiers. Choose the ports you are assigned to, distribute your ships between them (by hand or at random), and travel from one to another, either with a quick crossing or by sailing there yourself.
- **Walkable headquarters** — Visit the staff headquarters on foot: entrance hall, the admiral's office, the operations room, the CROSS rescue centre, the assignments office and the hall of honour.
- **Free game speed** — Choose ×1 to ×32 at any time, even in combat. Fast transit (Shift+T) sails your ship to the objective and hands control back at the first event.
- **Cutscenes** — Departure, end of mission, port arrival, crossings and enemy ships going down. Press Space to skip, or turn them off in the settings.
- **Living ships and ports** — Sailors on deck, pedestrians and gulls on the quays, dolphins at sea.
- **Service jeep** — Drive a navy jeep around the quays (up to about 66 km/h), take timed courier runs from the harbour office, and step out straight into a building.
- **Realistic ports** — Fenders, ladders, mooring lines, lamps that light up at night, a container yard, fuel tanks, a guard post, parked cars, port trucks and forklifts, fishing boats and harbour sounds.
- **Fleet overview** — See where each of your ships is berthed, even in another port, on the sea chart and in the "My fleet" panel (F).
- **Commercial traffic everywhere** — Cargo ships, tankers, container ships and ferries follow real shipping lanes across the whole map.
- **Special events at sea** — A dismasted yacht, drifting containers, a fin whale, a violent squall, smuggled bales: optional objectives with rewards.
- **New missions** — Hydrographic survey, cable-ship protection and a ditched helicopter (survivors, then the flight recorder).
- **Smarter characters** — Messages understand far more questions (fleet locations, weather forecast, repairs, upgrades, prices, ranks, mission tips, stories, jokes) and more ways of giving orders to allied ships.
- **Player comfort** — Settings panel (volume, voices, mouse sensitivity, larger text), photo mode (F8), on-screen objective marker, and career export/import as a `.json` file.

## 🌊 Setting

The game takes place in the Mediterranean Sea, where naval operations, missions, and communications shape your experience.

You are responsible for managing your vessel and carrying out your assigned objectives while reacting to events as they unfold.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

The game is currently distributed as an HTML file.

### Run locally

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Locate `Flotte d'Ysmar.html`.
3. Open the file in a modern web browser.
4. Start a mission and explore the available systems.

No traditional installation should be necessary for the standalone HTML version. Additional requirements may apply if the project uses external resources.
